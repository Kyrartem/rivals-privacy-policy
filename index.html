Privacy Policy

For FameRivals, an Android application published by Kyrat.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Scope and developer information

This Privacy Policy explains how Kyrat, an independent team of application developers (referred to as “we”, “us”, or “the developer”), handles information in connection with the Android application FameRivals (the “App”).

The App’s display name may change over time. Its permanent Android package identifier is com.rbx.sandbox.reward. This Privacy Policy applies to the App identified by that package identifier, regardless of any future changes to its display name.

Data processing depends on the App version, the features you use, your device settings, and your region. A third-party service mentioned below may not be active in every version of the App. We review the information in this policy as integrations change.

2. Information collection and use

You do not need to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or personal email address merely to install and use the basic features of the App. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond to your request.

Where supported, you may optionally sign in using the Google account associated with Google Play. This is not required to use the App's basic features. If you choose to sign in, the Google sign-in service may provide the App with an account identifier and limited profile information (such as a display name or email address, depending on the sign-in method and permissions). This information is used to enable authentication and account-related features. We do not receive your Google account password.

Depending on the App and its integrated third-party software development kits (SDKs), certain technical information may be collected or processed automatically:

  • Network information: IP address, network connection details, and approximate region inferred from the IP address.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, device language, and technical configuration.
  • Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (when available and permitted), app installation identifiers, and other identifiers used for advertising, measurement, security, or diagnostics.
  • App usage: app sessions, screens or features used, timestamps, and interaction or performance events.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error messages, technical logs, and stability information.

The App does not request your device's precise GPS location for its basic operation. However, network and advertising providers may infer an approximate location from an IP address or similar network information. We do not ask users to provide sensitive personal details to use the basic features of the App.

3. Why information is processed

We and our service providers may process the data described above to:

  • provide app functionality and deliver requested content or streams;
  • identify technical problems, investigate crashes, and improve reliability;
  • display advertisements, measure ad performance, and support advertising-related fraud prevention;
  • understand app performance and usage patterns to improve the App; and
  • respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App may use third-party services, including SDKs that process technical information directly from your device. Depending on its configuration, these services may include:

  • Google Play services for Android platform functionality and optional Google account sign-in, where supported. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) for reporting crashes and diagnosing stability issues, where integrated. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions) for advertising mediation and monetization, where enabled. See CAS.AI Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising network partners connected through the App's advertising stack, which may include Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), AppLovin, Unity Ads, Pangle, and Meta Audience Network if enabled for that App. These providers may receive device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, reporting, and fraud prevention. See the respective providers' privacy notices for information on their practices.

Advertising partners may process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may be transmitted to these providers for advertising and related operations; this should not be understood to mean that all information is shared only in anonymized or aggregated form. We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

Advertising may be contextual or personalized depending on available settings, regional requirements, user choices, and the providers used. Where required by applicable law, consent must be requested before the relevant personal-data processing takes place. You can also manage available advertising preferences in Android settings.

5. Streaming and external links

The App may display or play content provided by third-party websites, streaming servers, or content providers. When you request such content, your device may connect to those providers, which may receive technical network information such as your IP address in order to deliver the content. Their handling of this information is governed by their own policies.

The App may also contain links to external websites. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party sites and recommend that you review their privacy notices.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The App does not require traditional browser cookies for its basic functionality. Third-party SDKs and embedded web content, where used, may rely on device identifiers, local storage, cookies, or similar technologies for analytics, app operation, or advertising. The exact technologies depend on the provider and the App's configuration.

7. Log data and diagnostics

When the App crashes or encounters a technical error, diagnostic information may be processed by integrated services such as Firebase Crashlytics. Such records can include device and operating system details, the App version, timestamps, crash traces, and installation identifiers. We use this information to investigate and resolve problems.

8. Data retention and deletion

We retain information we directly receive, such as support correspondence, only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, resolve problems, or meet legal obligations. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed for those purposes, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to their own retention periods and deletion procedures. For example, Firebase Crashlytics generally retains crash traces and related identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal, as described in Firebase's documentation.

To request access to or deletion of information we control, email strmsupport@gmail.com and identify the App. We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need reasonable information to verify a request, and certain records may need to be retained where required by law. If the information is controlled by an independent provider, you may also need to use that provider's privacy controls or contact that provider directly.

Uninstalling the App stops new data collection by it, but does not automatically delete information that has already been transmitted to us or third-party providers.

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard data we control, including access restrictions and encrypted transmission where supported. Third-party service providers are responsible for their own security measures. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and adjust available advertising privacy settings in Android.
  • Where the App provides a consent or privacy-options interface, you can use it to manage the choices offered there.
  • You can disable notifications for the App in your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop its future operation on your device.
  • Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data we control, or object to certain processing. Contact us using the address below.

Changing advertising preferences does not necessarily disable all advertising, security, or technical processing.

Advertising services used in the App must be configured consistently with its actual target audience and applicable children's privacy requirements.
